05 PROCESS

How a project runs.

Most work starts by making an unclear task concrete enough to test. Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01 / SCOPE

    Write it down first

    We agree on the inputs, the output you want and how you will judge it done. One page, before any work starts.

  2. 02 / BUILD SMALL

    Show something early

    I build on a sample of your data first, show you the result, then run it on the full thing.

  3. 03 / HAND OVER

    You can run it without me

    Code, a short run guide and a recorded walkthrough, so the work does not depend on me being around.

  4. 04 / SUPPORT

    Fixes are included

    Defects in what I delivered get fixed. Changes and ongoing maintenance are agreed separately.

HOW I HANDLE YOUR DATA
  • I start with sample or anonymised data wherever possible.
  • Your data stays in the systems we agree on. I do not paste it into third-party AI tools unless you have approved that.
  • I am happy to sign an NDA. Ownership of the delivered work is agreed in writing before we start.
